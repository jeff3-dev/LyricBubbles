LyricBubbles
============

LyricBubbles shows the lyrics of whatever Spotify is playing,
in time with the song, in different visual styles.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A Mac running macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.
- The Spotify app for Mac (not the web player), signed in and playing a song.

INSTALL
1. Drag LyricBubbles into the Applications folder.
   (From the .zip: double-click it, then drag LyricBubbles into Applications.)
2. Open LyricBubbles from Applications. The first time, macOS will say it
   "can't be opened" or "could not verify" it. That's expected: this app isn't
   from the App Store or notarized by Apple. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about
   LyricBubbles and click "Open Anyway". Enter your password, then click
   "Open Anyway" once more.
4. When LyricBubbles asks to control Spotify, click OK. It needs this to know
   which song is playing and where you are in it.

You only do steps 2 to 4 once. After that it opens like any other app.

If "Open Anyway" doesn't show up, open Terminal and paste this, then try again:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LyricBubbles.app

USING IT
- Play a song in Spotify, then pick a style on the dashboard.
- Hover over a style window for the Dashboard and full screen buttons, and for
  play/pause and skip controls (Settings can show them always or hide them).
  Press Esc to leave full screen.
- Settings (Cmd-,) has controls for each style.
- If lyrics run early or late: Cmd-[ and Cmd-] nudge them.

GOOD TO KNOW
- LyricBubbles is an independent project. It isn't made, endorsed or supported
  by Spotify or LRCLIB.
- Lyrics come from LRCLIB and belong to their songwriters and publishers. They're
  shown for personal use. Some songs have no synced lyrics.
- The app never records or saves audio.

UPDATING
Quit LyricBubbles, then drag the new version into Applications and choose Replace.
macOS may ask again to let it control Spotify.

UNINSTALL
Drag LyricBubbles from Applications to the Trash.
